# Evaluation evidence

The [published index](index.md) and [latest scorecard](latest.md) contain an
**historical, incomplete August 2026 campaign**, fingerprint
`f8d6f3b6f3e0348c75a194bf`. “Latest” is a publication alias, not a claim that
these results measure the current runtime. The [machine-readable scorecard](latest.json)
and [sanitized provenance](latest_provenance.json) retain the recorded conditions.

The campaign is missing complete reviewed retrieval relevance labels. Its
semantic dimensions contain errors or skips with unavailable scores; accounting
for a judge job does not establish answer quality. Historical offline trace
configuration can remain unverified. These artifacts show the reporting system
and observed limitations; they do not establish current answer quality, a graph
advantage, production readiness, or a controlled before/after improvement.

Retrieval evidence, generated-answer assessment, and operational behavior have
separate contracts and denominators. The scorecard preserves missing coverage,
errors, and N/A values. No new measurements or M2 comparisons accompany M1.
